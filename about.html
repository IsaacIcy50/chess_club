About Oakland Tech Chess

Our history, mission, activities, leadership team, and tournament highlights

Our Mission

Founded by Oakland Tech students passionate about both classical over-the-board craftsmanship and modern analytical chess, the Oakland Tech Chess Club fosters critical thinking, sportsmanship, and universal chess accessibility for all Tech students. We meet every Friday during lunch and run our club for everyone.

Design & Theory Collaboration: Our futuristic neo-matrix visual style paired with classic hardwood textures and game theory workshops are crafted in collaboration with Hera.

What We Do

Casual Free-Play

Drop-in unrated games and beginner guidance every Friday during lunch.

Blitz & Rapid Mini-Tournaments

5+0 Blitz and 10+0 Rapid Swiss tournaments with digital chess clocks.

Opening Theory Workshops

Interactive breakdowns of the Italian Game, Ruy Lopez, Queen's Gambit, and Sicilian Defense.

Endgame Study

King opposition, pawn promotion races, and essential checkmate patterns.
