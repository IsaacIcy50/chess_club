OPENING CONCEPT

The Italian Game (1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4)

Demonstrates central control, rapid minor-piece development, and diagonal pressure on f7.

CLASSIC TACTIC

The Royal Knight Fork (1. Nxc7+)

Knight on c7 simultaneously checks the King on e8 and attacks the Rook on a8.
